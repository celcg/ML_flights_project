# Scripts

- `audit_new_flight_data.py` — checks monthly schemas and null patterns in the expanded dataset.
- `download_weatherdata.py` — downloads a small METAR/SPECI extract for future weather experiments.
- `prepare_business_report_assets.py` — creates reusable business tables, rankings, maps and figures.
- `run_business_eda.py` — runs the complete business analysis without Jupyter.
- `run_t60_25pct.py` — reproduces the low-memory 25% Ridge scaling experiment.

One-off Word generators were removed after their final documents were produced; the retained deliverables are in `doc/`.
